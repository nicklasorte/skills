# Citations, full filings, and procedure

Part of the ntia-fcc-filing-voice skill. Read section 11 when drafting footnotes and section 12 in Assemble mode. Section numbers match the SKILL.md contents list.

## 11. Citations

Write the full citation on first reference with a defined short form in parentheses. Use the short form after that, and *Id.* for the immediately preceding source. CFR citations should be to spectrum-related parts (section 1); if a non-spectrum part seems necessary, flag it.

**Rules:**

- Never invent a volume, page, paragraph number, FCC number, docket, footnote designator, or date. Verify with web tools when available. If a detail can't be verified, use `[CITE: <what's needed>]` and flag it.
- Check CFR citations against the current eCFR, since spectrum rules and the allocation table change often.
- Use ¶ for paragraph pin cites in Commission items and "at <page>" for filings, letters, and reports.
- Introduce supporting authority with *See*, *See, e.g.*, *See also*, or *Cf.* as the support warrants. Italicize signals and case names.
- Add "available at <URL>" for NTIA reports, ITU material, and web-only documents.

## 12. Full filings and procedure

### Caption

```
Before the
FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION
Washington, DC 20554

In the Matter of                    )
                                    )
<Proceeding Name>                   )   <Docket type> Docket No. <XX-XXX>
                                    )

COMMENTS OF THE
NATIONAL TELECOMMUNICATIONS AND INFORMATION ADMINISTRATION
```

Use REPLY COMMENTS, COMMENTS ON PETITION FOR WAIVER, PETITION FOR RECONSIDERATION, PETITION FOR STAY, or similar as the filing requires.

### Opening paragraph

The National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA), on behalf of the Executive Branch, respectfully submits these comments in response to the <document (short form)> in the above-captioned proceeding.[1] <One or two sentences stating NTIA's overall position and the federal equities the filing addresses.>

[1] NTIA is the Executive Branch agency principally responsible for advising the President on telecommunications and information policies and for presenting Executive Branch views on telecommunications matters to the Commission. *See* 47 U.S.C. § 902(b)(2)(D), (J).

Put the full citation to the Commission document in footnote 2.

### Body

Roman-numeral sections, lettered subsections, run-in headings for parallel items. Put detailed technical material (assignment data, link budgets, coordination procedures) in lettered attachments and summarize each in the body.

### Conclusion

Insert `* * *`, then one paragraph restating NTIA's role as the agency that presents Executive Branch views, its readiness to keep working with the Commission, and the outcome it supports. For a short filing: "NTIA respectfully requests that the Commission consider the views expressed herein and looks forward to working with the Commission to achieve the objectives of this proceeding."

### Procedural checklist

Check these when assembling a full filing, and verify each against the current rules:

- **Table of contents and summary:** Filings longer than 10 pages need a table of contents with page references and a summary. 47 CFR § 1.49(b)-(c). The summary should seldom exceed one page and never two for filings of 10 to 25 pages, and seldom two and never five for longer filings. These pages don't count against page limits.
- **Signature:** 47 CFR § 1.52.
- **Comment and reply deadlines:** from the Federal Register notice or any extension order. 47 CFR §§ 1.415, 1.419.
- **Reconsideration deadlines and page limits:** section 8.
- **Ex parte content:** 47 CFR § 1.1206(b).
- **Docket numbers:** every docket in the caption and in each ECFS submission.
