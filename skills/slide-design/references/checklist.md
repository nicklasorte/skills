# Review checklist

Run this on every slide or figure before you hand it over. Render slides to
images and look at them; don't review from code or XML alone. Fix every
"no," then check again.

## Deck structure

- [ ] The slide titles, read alone in order, tell the whole story
      (horizontal logic).
- [ ] The ask or bottom line comes first for leadership decks.
- [ ] Everything on each slide supports that slide's title (vertical logic).
- [ ] Method, full data, and extra scenarios are in labeled backup slides.

## Message

- [ ] The title is a full sentence that states the takeaway.
- [ ] The slide or figure makes one point.
- [ ] Someone glancing for three seconds knows where to look first.
- [ ] The visual proves the headline. Nothing on it is unrelated to the
      headline.

## Text

- [ ] Presented mode: the headline is 12 words or fewer and fits on two
      lines, and other text is 15 words or fewer, with no bullet lists.
- [ ] Read-ahead mode: each page stands on its own, with a bold lead-in on
      each text block and the limits in `read-ahead.md`.
- [ ] Acronyms are spelled out on first use or covered in a glossary.
- [ ] Numbers are rounded to the precision the decision needs.

## Layout

- [ ] Every element aligns to the grid and to other elements.
- [ ] Everything sits inside the layout's content area, and nothing covers
      the logo, footer, or master graphics.
- [ ] At least a third of the content area is empty.
- [ ] Titles sit in the same position as on the other slides.
- [ ] Text uses only the NTIA type scale on slides (32 pt headline, 20 pt
      body, 16 pt labels, 96–120 pt hero number, 11 pt source), and 8 pt or
      larger at final size in report figures.

## Color

- [ ] The chart uses mid gray for context and light blue for the point,
      with purple only for a required second highlight.
- [ ] Color meanings are consistent across the deck or report.
- [ ] Text contrast is at least 4.5:1, and data marks are at least 3:1.
- [ ] The chart still works in grayscale and without red-green
      distinction.

## Chart

- [ ] The chart type fits the message (see `chart-types.md`).
- [ ] Legends are replaced with direct labels where possible.
- [ ] Gridlines, borders, tick marks, and backgrounds are removed or
      lightened.
- [ ] There's no 3D, shadow, or gradient.
- [ ] One to three annotations prove the headline (up to five in read-ahead
      mode).
- [ ] Maps use a deliberate classification method, four to six classes, and
      rates (not counts) for fill color.
- [ ] Tables right-align numbers, show units once, and have no merged cells.
- [ ] Axis labels are plain words with units, and no text is rotated.

## Honesty

- [ ] Bars start at zero, and there are no dual y-axes or broken bar axes.
- [ ] Log scales and decibels are labeled or translated.
- [ ] Side-by-side charts and maps share scales and extents.
- [ ] Uncertainty is shown when it would change the decision, in plain
      terms for leadership (counts, ranges in words, quantile dots).
- [ ] A source line is present, with "Preliminary" if it applies.

## Section 508

- [ ] Every slide has a unique title in the title placeholder.
- [ ] Reading order is set in the Selection Pane.
- [ ] Every chart, map, image, and icon group has alt text that states the
      takeaway; decorative shapes are marked decorative.
- [ ] Color is never the only cue, and contrast passes.
- [ ] The file name is descriptive, and the Accessibility Checker shows no
      errors.

## Nick's standing rules

- [ ] Icons and diagrams use native shapes only.
- [ ] No animation or transitions.
- [ ] The deck is built on the NTIA template, with the master, layouts,
      logo, and footer intact, and only NTIA colors and theme fonts.
- [ ] DRAFT//PRE-DECISIONAL appears on every slide or page (header and
      footer in documents). No CUI markings.
- [ ] Every repeated number matches the numbers ledger: same value, same
      rounding, same units, and totals add up. Rates use one denominator.
- [ ] Derived numbers and icon counts were computed from exact values by
      running the arithmetic.

## Report and doc figures only

- [ ] Report figure images hold only the chart and annotations; the title,
      caption, and source line are document text. Standalone images carry
      the title, source line, and marking.
- [ ] The figure is numbered and referenced in the text before it appears.
- [ ] The caption gives method details in one to three sentences.
- [ ] The figure is sized to the column, and fonts are 8–10 pt at final size.
- [ ] The export is vector (EMF, SVG, or PDF) or PNG at 300 dpi.
- [ ] Alt text states the takeaway and the chart type.
