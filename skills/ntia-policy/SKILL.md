# NTIA Policy

## Scope and intake
Establish document type, audience, decision, requested agency position, applicable authority and record. Distinguish agency proposals from adopted requirements and third-party assertions.

## Method
Verify applicable statutes, FCC rules, orders, notices, and NTIA public materials against primary sources. Preserve allocations, assignments, authorizations, frequency ranges, footnotes, protection criteria, and mandatory versus permissive language. Never invent concurrence or legal conclusions.

## Deliverables
Produce a source-linked issue outline, precise draft or redline, open policy questions, and a clearance checklist. Separate confirmed facts, proposed policy, and unresolved issues.

## Quality and safety gates
- Cite exact accessible source versions and locations for material claims; distinguish facts, assumptions, proposals, and unknowns.
- No internal documents, agency-specific positions, source code, or restricted data are bundled in this public skill.
- Missing evidence blocks only unsupported conclusions; continue independent draft work with explicit gaps.
- Use NTIA-Review for cross-document review and NTIA Quality Gate for an explicit advisory readiness assessment when those skills are available.
- Human review and applicable institutional clearance remain required; this skill does not authorize publication or filing.
