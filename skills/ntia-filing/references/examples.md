## 18. Worked example

**Input (Expand mode):**
> FCC wants fixed CZ distances in the NPRM. bad idea. DoD systems move around, aggregate is what matters. our analysis: zones vary w/ deployment density/power. want NTIA to host + update zones like CBRS / 37 GHz. ok w/ auction if zones are dynamic.

**Output:**

The Commission proposes to establish fixed-radius coordination zones to protect federal operations in the band.[1] NTIA supports making the band available for non-federal use, provided that protection of federal operations rests on coordination zones that NTIA can update, rather than on fixed protection distances codified in the Commission's rules. Federal operations in the band include [CONFIRM: DoD system types], whose operating locations change over time. The risk of harmful interference to these systems depends on the aggregate emissions of nearby non-federal stations, not on the distance to any single transmitter. NTIA's preliminary analysis indicates that the protection distance a federal site requires varies with the density, power, and antenna characteristics of surrounding non-federal deployments.[2] NTIA therefore recommends that the Commission adopt a coordination framework similar to those used in the 3550-3650 MHz and 37-38 GHz bands, under which NTIA defines, publishes, and updates coordination zones as federal and non-federal operations evolve.[3] This approach would protect federal missions while allowing non-federal licensees to deploy wherever aggregate interference remains below the applicable protection criteria.

Footnotes
[1] [CITE: NPRM title, docket, FCC number, release date], ¶ [CONFIRM].
[2] [CITE: NTIA analysis title, report number or attachment, date].
[3] *See Amendment of the Commission's Rules with Regard to Commercial Operations in the 3550-3650 MHz Band*, GN Docket No. 12-354, Report and Order and Second Further Notice of Proposed Rulemaking, 30 FCC Rcd 3959 (2015); [CITE: 37-38 GHz coordination order].

Flags
- Footnotes 1 and 2 need the NPRM and analysis details; footnote 3 needs the 37-38 GHz order and pin cites.
- Confirm the DoD system types are releasable before filing.
- Preservation: if the analysis isn't filed, attach it or cite where it's in the record, so the aggregate-interference argument is preserved.
