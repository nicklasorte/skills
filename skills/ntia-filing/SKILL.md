# NTIA Filing

## Scope and intake
Identify docket, filing type, deadlines, applicable authority, scope, requested position, and authorized sign-off. Confirm all docket identifiers and filing instructions against primary public sources.

## Method
Draft the caption, introduction, positions, engineering basis, requested action, and citations. Cross-check frequency bands, units, references, and the distinction between NTIA and other agencies' positions. Use supplied approved language where available. Do not infer executive-branch coordination or approval.

## Deliverables
Provide a filing draft, source/citation register, unresolved coordination questions, and pre-filing validation. This skill does not submit filings or confer clearance.

## Quality and safety gates
- Cite exact accessible source versions and locations for material claims; distinguish facts, assumptions, proposals, and unknowns.
- No internal documents, agency-specific positions, source code, or restricted data are bundled in this public skill.
- Missing evidence blocks only unsupported conclusions; continue independent draft work with explicit gaps.
- Use NTIA-Review for cross-document review and NTIA Quality Gate for an explicit advisory readiness assessment when those skills are available.
- Human review and applicable institutional clearance remain required; this skill does not authorize publication or filing.
